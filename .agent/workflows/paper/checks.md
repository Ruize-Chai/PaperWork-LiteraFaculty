# Paper workflow checks

Use this checklist after substantive writing. Report unknown or unautomated checks as such.

## Scientific consistency

- Each strong claim has support in the claim registry or explicit source mapping.
- Conditions, scope, limitations, and unresolved caveats remain visible.
- Values, experiment identifiers, data versions, and result states agree with current source records.
- No contradiction was introduced between sections, abstract, conclusion, and response letter.

## Structure and assets

- Section, equation, figure, table, and appendix references resolve and numbering is consistent.
- Referenced figure/table/supplement assets exist; captions match their current evidence and do not overstate it.
- Existing final figures were reused unless the plan calls for regeneration; new output passed visual QA.

## Citations and review

- Citation keys resolve in the repository's bibliography system; no placeholder or fabricated entry remains.
- Background citations are distinct from evidence for this work's results.
- Review concerns retain explicit resolution states and evidence; disagreement or new validation is recorded.

## Version and submission

- Manuscript, Paper IR/project status, experiment state, and response-letter versions are compatible.
- Applicable venue and submission requirements were checked against repository metadata or supplied instructions.

`paper workflow postwrite` performs the available deterministic Paper IR audit and emits this broader review checklist. The checklist is not a semantic proof or a substitute for visual, literature, or scientific review.
